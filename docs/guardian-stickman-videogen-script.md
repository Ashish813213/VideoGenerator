# Guardian Stickman Presenter Test Script

Use this as a VideoGen input when testing the interactive presenter character.
It keeps the emotional Guardian story, but phrases the narration so a small
stickman explainer can guide the viewer without replacing the cinematic visuals.

## VideoGen Script

What if feeling unsafe could instantly connect you with trusted help nearby?

Meet Guardian, a community-driven women safety app designed for moments when
you do not want to walk alone.

It does not replace police or emergency services. It adds one more layer of
support by connecting you with verified nearby volunteers while your emergency
contacts stay informed.

A young woman leaves work in the evening. The street is quiet. Nothing dramatic
has happened, but a nearby group makes her feel uneasy. Sometimes, feeling
unsafe is enough to change your entire journey.

She opens Guardian. A clean map appears with nearby verified volunteers, distance
markers, green verification badges, and one clear button: Request Assistance.

With one tap, her live location is shared, nearby volunteers are notified, and
her emergency contacts receive an update.

Cut to the community. A young female volunteer and a middle-aged male volunteer
receive the same alert: Nearby Assistance Request.

Their profiles show verified identity, community volunteer status, and completed
background verification. One volunteer accepts and starts walking toward the
shared location.

The volunteer meets her in a well-lit public area. There is no confrontation and
no violence, just calm reassurance. She smiles with relief, and they walk toward
a safer, busier street.

Guardian works because safety grows stronger when communities stand together.
Verified volunteers, families, neighborhoods, and everyday people become part of
a trusted local support network.

Never walk alone. Help is nearby.

Guardian. Community verified. Safer together.

## Presenter Direction

- Style: clean stickman presenter
- Tone: calm, reassuring, premium
- Gestures: alert during uncertainty, point during app UI, reassure during safe arrival, celebrate during community montage
- Speech bubbles: short phrases only, such as "One tap", "Verified nearby", "Location shared", "Safer together"
- Avoid: violence, weapons, exaggerated rescue claims, police replacement language
